Projects

Metro Van Transit Pulse→

Tools: Python, Pandas, GeoPandas, NetworkX, Shiny

A transit connectivity visualizer with fun sliders allowing you to modify system parameters. Test system resilience against infrastructure failure, examine the impacts of limited mobility, or just mess around and look at the cool blobs!

UBC SkyTrain Extension Study→

Tools: Aimsun, Python, QGIS

Should Translink have built the Millenium Line extension straight to UBC? Well, obviously I'm a little biased. This study compares the interim solution to what will be eventually built and gave me a chance to do a full modeling project on my own.

RFEM Steel Bridge Dashboard →

Tools: Python, Pandas, Dlubal RFEM API

A dashboard that streamlines the design and analysis workflow for competition trusses, eliminating the need for manual copy pasting to and from Excel and hugely speeding up the design process.
